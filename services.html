Recurring Winter Service

Priced per clearing

Recurring service is for customers who want Pioneer to return through the winter rather than request each clearing separately.

  • Property and service preferences recorded at enrollment
  • Driveway, sidewalk, and salting preferences can be retained
  • Route availability must be confirmed before enrollment
  • Storm-response terms are provided when recurring service is accepted

What standard pricing assumes

Homepage prices are starting prices for typical residential properties. Final pricing can change for larger areas, unusual layouts, difficult access, unusually heavy work, or requests outside the primary Toledo route.

What is not automatically included

Unlisted areas, extensive hand work, special ice treatment, hauling snow away from the property, and work outside the agreed scope are not automatically included in standard driveway pricing.

Service area

Toledo ZIP codes beginning with 436 are the primary service area. Nearby 435 ZIP codes can submit requests but require route-capacity confirmation before service is accepted.

Before service begins

A website request is a request for service, not an automatic booking. Pioneer confirms route availability, service scope, and final pricing before accepting the job.
